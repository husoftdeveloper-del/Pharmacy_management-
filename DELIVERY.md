## Purchase form fix

Build 14 fixes `Invalid record ID` when receiving a purchase. The purchase form now explicitly maps supplier, medicine, location, invoice, payment method and account fields to the secure IPC contract. The same naming mismatch was corrected in related payment, expense, staff, transfer, stock-count and prescription forms. Cash/bank account choices now follow the selected payment method, and credit purchases hide the irrelevant account field.

## Barcode update

Barcode Management now has an icon button inside the barcode input. Clicking it generates a unique `MED-…` barcode through the secure main process, fills the input, and shows the CODE128-B preview immediately. Choose a medicine and press Save to assign it; Print remains available after preview. The action is permission-checked in the main process and covered by database and Electron smoke tests.
